Read my curatorial rationale:

"As an artist, my intention is to bring viewers to their senses while inspiring them to connect with my art on an emotional and spiritual level. My aim is to help slow down the pace of humanity and encourage viewers to explore deeper meanings in life by capturing the small moments that usually go unnoticed.

My artwork explores mature themes of relationships and emotion, primarily aimed at young adults. I hope it offers a sense of comfort and understanding while encouraging viewers to acknowledge their emotions, reflect on their experiences, and appreciate perspectives beyond their own."
